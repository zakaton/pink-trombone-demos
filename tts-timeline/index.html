Top lane: drag a block's right edge to change its hold, the white diamond to move the transition point (the hold adjusts so the block keeps its length), and drag up or down inside a block for intensity. Bottom lane: drag a dot up or down to pin that block to a manual pitch (filled dot); the other blocks (hollow dots) automatically hold the last manual pitch or interpolate toward the next one, per the auto pitch setting. Double-click a dot to make it automatic again. Space plays the selected word, Shift+Space plays the whole phrase, click a word's name to play it, arrow keys move the selection (left/right) or nudge intensity (up/down), and Shift+up/down nudges pitch.
