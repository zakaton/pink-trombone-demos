One of you creates a room and sends the other the code or invite link. Only Samuel's articulation data is streamed (a few kilobytes a second) between your two browsers; no audio is sent. Each of you then hears the other's voice through your own Pink Trombone tab, never your own.

Your voice (sent to your partner)

Lines are every frame Samuel measured; the dots are the keys that were actually sent. Flat stretches and straight slopes need very few. Wear headphones: Pink Trombone's sound would otherwise be picked up by your microphone and sent back to your partner.

Your partner's voice (played on Pink Trombone)

Open the Pink Trombone page in another tab and click it once so it can make sound. Transpose and tract length are applied here, to what you hear, so each of you can set them for the voice you listen to.
