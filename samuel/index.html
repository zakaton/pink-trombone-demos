Open the Pink Trombone page in another tab and click it once so it can make sound. Space plays and pauses (when you aren't typing) and picks up where it left off; the stop button rewinds. Click anywhere on the graphs to move the playback position, then press play or Space to start from there. Hover the graphs to read the values at any moment, or click and drag across them to hear that moment live (with "listen to" set to recording or both, you hear the original audio at that point too). For audio files and recordings, "listen to" lets you hear the original, the articulation, or both at once; nudge the sync offset if they sound slightly apart. Transpose shifts the pitch of what is sent to Pink Trombone (the graphs keep showing Samuel's own pitch). Samuel's articulation is built for a vocal tract length of 44; a different length scales the tongue, constriction and lip positions with it. Simplify drops keyframes that a straight line between their neighbours already reproduces, which makes the result lighter to play and easier to edit in the timeline.
