Open the Pink Trombone page in another tab and click it once. Double-click a lane to add a key, drag keys to move them, drag on empty space to box-select (shift adds), click a track name to select its keys, A selects everything, Alt+A or Esc clears, Delete removes, I keys every track at the playhead, Space plays, wheel zooms, shift+wheel pans, Ctrl/⌘Z undoes. Loading an utterance JSON from the text-to-speech page works too.
