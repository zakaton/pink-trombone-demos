Open the Pink Trombone page in another tab and click it once. Keys are bezier F-curve points (tick free handles to make every dragged handle independent; Ctrl/⌘+wheel or the slider resizes the lanes): select one to show its handles and drag them (auto handles become aligned, vector become free). Double-click a lane to add a key, drag or box-select keys, G grabs and S scales the selection (X or Y to constrain, click or Enter confirms, Esc cancels), T and V pick interpolation and handle type, A selects all, I keys every track at the playhead, Space plays, Home fits the view, wheel zooms, shift+wheel pans, C cleans up the selected keys (each parameter on its own; the tolerance is a percentage of that parameter's range), Ctrl/⌘Z undoes. Load or record an audio reference to see its waveform above the lanes: scrub it, choose which to hear (articulation, recording or both) and nudge the sync offset until they line up.
